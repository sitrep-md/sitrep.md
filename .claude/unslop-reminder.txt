[unslop: mandatory] All prose you emit: chat replies, plans, summaries,
commit messages, docs, code comments, UI and marketing copy. Full rules
(31 patterns): .claude/skills/unslop/SKILL.md.

No em dashes or en dashes. Comma or full stop; parentheses are not a substitute.
Banned words: additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, foster, garner,
interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament,
underscore, vibrant, leverage, utilize, facilitate, numerous.
Banned metaphor nouns: substrate, wedge, vector, locus, nexus, surface,
primitive, harness, scaffolding, modality, paradigm, flywheel, north star,
endgame. Use the concrete word.
Say "is" or "has", not "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features".
No "not just X, but Y". No forced threes. No "from X to Y" non-ranges.
Sentence case headings, straight quotes, no emoji, no bold on every noun,
no "**Label:** label restated" bullets.
Active voice with a named actor. One idea per sentence. Cut adverbs, hedges,
filler. No "Great question", "You're absolutely right", "I hope this helps".
Name the mechanism, the file or the number, never the feeling. If a sentence
would read the same in another project's docs, cut it.
Self-audit: what makes this obviously AI generated? Fix that.
